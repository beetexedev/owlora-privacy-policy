Owlora Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 23, 2026

Applies to: the Owlora: Your Study Companion app

The short version

  • Your notes, flashcards, quizzes, and study plans stay only on your device unless you buy Cloud Sync.
  • We don't run ads and don't use third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • Signing in uses your Google account's name, email, and photo — nothing else from Google.
  • Scanning a document or asking Owlora to summarize, format, or generate flashcards/quizzes sends that content to our servers to produce the result.
  • Purchases (credits, Cloud Sync) go through Google Play and RevenueCat — we never see your card details.
  • You can ask us to delete your account and data at any time.

1. Information we collect

Owlora is built local-first: your study content lives in a database on your own device by default. Anything not listed below, we don't collect.

Account information — When you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, profile photo, and a unique Google account identifier. We use this to create and secure your account and to identify your data if you reinstall the app or switch devices.

Your study content — Notes, folders, flashcards, decks, quizzes, quiz answers, and study plans are stored locally on your device. If you purchase Cloud Sync, this same content is also stored on our servers so it can sync across your devices and survive a reinstall.

Scanned documents and photos — If you scan a document or photo to turn it into a note, the image is processed to extract its text — either on your device, or, for AI-assisted scans, uploaded to our servers for processing. Only the extracted text is kept as part of your note.

Content you ask Owlora to process with AI — When you ask Owlora to summarize a note, reformat it, or generate flashcards or quizzes from it, the relevant text is sent to our servers and processed — which may include passing it to a third-party AI provider — to produce the result.

Purchase information — In-app purchases (AI credits, OCR credits, Cloud Sync storage) are processed by Google Play Billing and RevenueCat, our subscription management provider. We receive purchase confirmations, product identifiers, and a RevenueCat-assigned identifier linked to your account. We never receive your card number or other payment details.

Device information — If you use Cloud Sync, we receive your device's name so you can tell which device made a given change — for example, "synced from Sarah's iPhone."

What we don't collect — Study reminders are scheduled and shown entirely on your device; we never see them. As of this policy's effective date, Owlora does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting SDKs, and we don't run ads.

2. How we use your information

  • To create, secure, and authenticate your account.
  • To store and sync your study content across your devices, if you've purchased Cloud Sync.
  • To generate the summaries, flashcards, quizzes, and scanned-text results you ask for.
  • To track and apply your AI/OCR credit balance and Cloud Sync storage.
  • To respond to support requests you send us.
  • To maintain the security of our service and prevent abuse.

4. Data storage and security

Your study content is stored locally in an on-device database by default. Access tokens are stored using your device's secure credential storage, not in plain text. Data sent to our servers travels over encrypted (HTTPS) connections. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information.

5. Data retention

Content stored only on your device remains there until you delete it or uninstall the app. Content stored on our servers because you've purchased Cloud Sync is retained for as long as your account is active, or until you ask us to delete it. We may retain limited information where necessary to comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, or enforce our agreements.

6. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete the personal information we hold about you. To exercise any of these rights — including deleting your account and all associated data — email us at the address below. We'll respond and complete verified deletion requests within a reasonable time, except where we're required to retain information by law.

7. Children's privacy

Owlora is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we'll delete it.

8. International data transfers

Your information may be processed in countries other than the one you live in, including the Philippines and the United States, wherever Owlora or the service providers listed above operate. By using Owlora, you consent to this transfer and processing.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Owlora changes. If we make material changes, we'll update the effective date above and, where appropriate, notify you in the app. Continued use of Owlora after a change means you accept the updated policy.

10. Contact us

Questions about this policy, or a request to access, correct, or delete your data? Reach us at beetexe.dev@gmail.com.
