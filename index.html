Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 1, 2026

Applies to: the Owlora: Your Study Companion app

The short version

  • Your notes, flashcards, quizzes, and study plans stay only on your device unless you subscribe to Cloud Sync.
  • We don't run ads and don't use third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • Signing in uses your Google account's name, email, and photo — nothing else from Google.
  • Scanning a document, generating notes from a topic, or asking Owlora to summarize, format, or create flashcards/quizzes sends that content to our servers and AI providers to produce the result.
  • Purchases (credits and the Cloud Sync subscription) go through Google Play and RevenueCat — we never see your card details.
  • You can delete your account and data from inside the app at any time.

What's changed in this version

  • Cloud Sync is now a yearly subscription, and we explain what happens to your backup if it ends.
  • You can now delete your account directly in the app (with a 30-day grace period).
  • We now name the providers that process your data, including our hosting, network, and AI providers.
  • We added details on device integrity checks, IP addresses, and usage records.

1. Information we collect

Owlora is built local-first: your study content lives in a database on your own device by default. Anything not listed below, we don't collect.

Account information — When you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, profile photo, and a unique Google account identifier. We use this to create and secure your account and to identify your data if you reinstall the app or switch devices.

Device integrity check — When you create a new account, the app asks Google Play Integrity to confirm the request comes from a genuine copy of Owlora on a real device. We receive only the resulting verdict, which we use to prevent fake and automated accounts.

Your study content — Notes, folders, flashcards, decks, quizzes, quiz answers, and study plans are stored locally on your device. If you subscribe to Cloud Sync, this same content is also stored on our servers so it can sync across your devices and survive a reinstall.

Scanned documents and photos — If you scan a document or photo to turn it into a note, the image is processed to extract its text — either on your device, or, for premium scans, uploaded to our servers and processed by Google Cloud Vision. Only the extracted text is kept as part of your note; we don't store the image.

Content you ask Owlora to process with AI — When you ask Owlora to summarize or reformat a note, generate flashcards or quizzes, or generate notes from a topic, the relevant text is sent to our servers and to our AI provider, OpenAI, to produce the result.

Purchase information — In-app purchases (AI credits, scan credits, and the Cloud Sync subscription) are processed by Google Play Billing and RevenueCat, our subscription management provider. We receive purchase confirmations, product identifiers, prices paid, subscription status and renewal dates, and a RevenueCat identifier linked to your account. We never receive your card number or other payment details.

Usage records — We keep a record of your credit balances and transactions, and of each AI or scan request (which feature was used, its size, and the credits it used), so we can charge credits correctly, show your history, and handle support requests.

Device information — If you use Cloud Sync, we receive your device's name so you can tell which device made a given change — for example, "synced from Maria's phone."

IP address and technical logs — Like any online service, our servers and our network provider process your IP address and basic request information when the app connects to us. We use this to keep the service secure, limit abuse, and fix problems.

What we don't collect — Study reminders are scheduled and shown entirely on your device; we never see them. As of this policy's effective date, Owlora does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting SDKs, and we don't run ads.

2. How we use your information

  • To create, secure, and authenticate your account.
  • To store and sync your study content across your devices while your Cloud Sync subscription is active.
  • To generate the summaries, notes, flashcards, quizzes, and scanned-text results you ask for.
  • To track your AI and scan credit balances and your Cloud Sync subscription status.
  • To respond to support requests you send us.
  • To maintain the security of our service and prevent fraud and abuse.

4. Data storage and security

Your study content is stored locally in an on-device database by default. Sign-in tokens are stored using your device's secure credential storage, not in plain text. Data sent to our servers travels over encrypted (HTTPS) connections. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information.

5. Data retention

Content stored only on your device remains there until you delete it or uninstall the app.

While you're subscribed to Cloud Sync, your synced content is kept on our servers.

If your Cloud Sync subscription ends, syncing stops, but your backed-up content stays on our servers so you can pick up where you left off if you subscribe again. It is deleted when you delete your account.

When you delete your account, it's deactivated immediately and permanently deleted after a 30-day grace period. At that point, your synced content is deleted and your account details (name, email, and photo) are removed. We keep anonymized records of purchases and credit transactions, which can no longer be linked to you, for accounting and legal purposes.

Deleted data may remain in our server backups for a short period before those backups are overwritten. We may also retain limited information where necessary to comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, or enforce our agreements.

6. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete the personal information we hold about you.

Deleting your account — You can delete your account from the app's account settings. Your account is deactivated immediately and permanently deleted after 30 days. If you change your mind, simply sign back in within those 30 days and the deletion is cancelled.

Other requests — To access, correct, or export your data, email us at the address below. We'll respond within a reasonable time, except where we're required to retain information by law.

Your subscription — You can cancel Cloud Sync at any time in Google Play. Cancelling stops future renewals; deleting your account doesn't cancel a subscription by itself, so please cancel it in Google Play too.

7. Children's privacy

Owlora is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we'll delete it.

8. International data transfers

Your information may be processed in countries other than the one you live in, including the Philippines and the United States, and wherever Owlora or the service providers listed above operate. By using Owlora, you consent to this transfer and processing.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Owlora changes. If we make material changes, we'll update the effective date above and, where appropriate, notify you in the app. Continued use of Owlora after a change means you accept the updated policy.
